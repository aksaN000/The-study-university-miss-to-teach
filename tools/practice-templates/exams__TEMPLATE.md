# Exam template

## Part N, <part title>: exam

Closed book. From memory. No terminal, no notes.

**Section A, recall.** 4 to 6 short-answer questions, one per major chapter.

**Section B, predict.** 2 to 3 "what happens if" traces through a mechanism.

**Section C, decompose.** A real command from this part, broken into every token, cold.

**Section D, teach it.** The hardest idea in the part, explained in writing to a junior.

**Grading.** Honestly, by ourselves. A shaky answer sends us back to that chapter first.
