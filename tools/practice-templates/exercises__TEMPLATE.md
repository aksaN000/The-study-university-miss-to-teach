# Exercise template

## Chapter NN: <chapter title>

**1. Do it again, alone.** Run the chapter's core command without looking back. Paste what you got.

**2. Predict, then check.** <Change one token or condition; predict the outcome before running it.>

**3. In your own words.** <One-paragraph conceptual question, answered as a notes.md entry.>

**4. Teach it.** Explain the idea to a junior who has never seen it. Any sentence that needs
"just trust me" is the part to re-read.

**Starting states.** Where an exercise needs a machine in a particular condition, ship it as
two folders next to the exercise: `broken/` (the failing setup the reader starts from) and
`fixed/` (one working answer, linked from `solutions/`). The reader diagnoses before reading.

**5. Break it a different way.** Find a different failure than the chapter's own, and explain
why it fails for a different reason.
